Egregore: Towards Shared Minds

Egregore is an agentic knowledge management system that builds itself: one memory your whole company shares, gathered from everyday work, kept in order, and handed to whoever needs it next.

or scroll to read
01

Every company knows more than any single member.

It lives in people's heads, inboxes, chats and meeting notes. Finding a piece of it usually means knowing whom to ask, and hoping they remember.

Hover over or tap anyone to see what they know.

02

That knowledge shrinks when someone leaves.

When a person moves on, the reasons behind their work go with them. Whoever takes their place starts from zero and spends months asking around.

03

Egregore learns from the work you already do.

Meetings, documents, messages and decisions all leave traces. Egregore keeps the useful parts as the work happens, so nobody has to stop and write things up for a wiki that goes stale.

04

It connects what belongs together.

A decision stays linked to the meeting where it was made, the people who made it and the work that followed. The memory sorts itself: what the company decided, what it knows about its customers, where each project stands, and what is still open.

Hover over or tap a dot to see what it is and whose it was.

05

Anyone can ask what the company knows.

Ask in plain words. The answer draws on everyone's work and shows where each part came from.

Try a question
Question

Why did we change our pricing last spring?

Answer

It was decided at the March planning review. The customer survey showed small teams leaving over the entry price, and the pricing analysis found that a lower first tier would pay for itself within a year.

Maya · review notesSam · customer surveyTom · pricing analysis
06

People come and go. What they learned stays.

When Sam leaves, Sam's share of the memory stays, still linked to everything it touched. The next hire starts on day one with what the company has learned, and can ask it anything.

07

It takes the shape of your company, and of each person in it.

Every team works its own way, and Egregore picks up those habits: what sales needs to remember differs from what product needs. Each person gets it the way they prefer, whether that is a quick answer in the chat they already use, a weekly summary, or silence until they ask.

08

You decide what it can see.

Private notes stay private. Each team chooses what it shares with the rest of the company. Everything Egregore holds can be traced to where it came from, and taken out again.

Hover over or tap a team to see what its members can see.

09

The longer it runs, the more it knows.

Every week of work adds to it. A company's knowledge stops scattering and starts to build on itself, and each new question gets a better answer than the last.
